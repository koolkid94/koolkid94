# Porcelain Flowers
A Fabric 1.21 mod (tentative, may change version) where the world comes alive. Plants and animals simulate trophic hierarchy, and villagers work the land they live. I've always wanted the Minecraft world to have the impression that it exists outside of just the player's view. This mod aims to add features to simulate various ecological cycles occuring in our world into Minecraft, with the aim of simulating a real ecosystem with systems that can be harnessed by players and villagers.
